## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Supported delivery channels

Relayform MUST present only Telegram, VK, MAX and e-mail as supported delivery channels in product, architecture, API-planning and marketing materials.

#### Scenario: Channel enumeration

- **WHEN** a user or implementer reads a Relayform channel list
- **THEN** it contains Telegram, VK, MAX and e-mail
- **AND THEN** it does not promise WhatsApp support.
